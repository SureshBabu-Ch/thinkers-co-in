Exact Degree of Freedom Mathematical Formulas for 1D Cavities

  • 1. Spatial Standing Waves in 1D: Inside a 1D cavity of length $L$ bounded by conducting walls at $x = 0$ and $x = L$, the spatial boundary conditions require $k = \frac{n\pi}{L}$ with $n \in \{1, 2, 3, \dots\}$. Unlike 2D lines or 3D planes, nodes in 1D are zero-dimensional discrete points where $E = 0$ constantly.
  • 2. Non-Degeneracy in 1D ($g_n = 1$): Each wavenumber $k$ corresponds uniquely to a single spatial harmonic. There are no permutation degeneracies in 1D, meaning every spatial state is strictly non-degenerate.
  • 3. Quadratic Degrees of Freedom per Mode ($f = 2$): Each standing wave mode acts as an autonomous harmonic oscillator. Its Hamiltonian contains 2 quadratic terms: electric field energy ($U_E \propto \int E^2 dx$) and magnetic field energy ($U_B \propto \int B^2 dx$). By equipartition, each quadratic coordinate receives $\frac{1}{2}k_B T$, yielding:
    \langle E_n \rangle = 2 \times \left(\frac{1}{2} k_B T\right) = k_B T
  • 4. 1D Density of States ($g(\nu) = \text{constant}$): The number of spatial modes with frequency below $\nu$ is $N(\nu) = \frac{2L}{c} \nu$. Differentiating yields a flat, frequency-independent density of states:
    g_{1\text{D}}(\nu) d\nu = \frac{2L}{c} d\nu = \text{constant} Comparing dimensions: $g_{1\text{D}}(\nu) \propto \nu^0$ (constant), $g_{2\text{D}}(\nu) \propto \nu^1$ (linear), and $g_{3\text{D}}(\nu) \propto \nu^2$ (quadratic ultraviolet catastrophe).
