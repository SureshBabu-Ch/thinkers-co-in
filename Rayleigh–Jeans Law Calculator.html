Note on Classical Equipartition Derivation: This calculation is performed purely according to the Rayleigh–Jeans Law (1900–1905). It relies strictly on classical electrodynamics (cavity mode density $\propto \lambda^{-4}$) and the classical equipartition theorem of energy ($\bar{\epsilon} = k_B T$). Comparing it against the real experimental blackbody observation demonstrates the classical failure known as the Ultraviolet Catastrophe.
