Why High Energy Cannot Sustain Itself (The Contrast Principle)

Collision Dissipation Sequence:
• Red (Fast) + Blue (Slow): Instant kinetic transfer → Both particles become Green (Medium Speed).
• Green + Green / Green + Blue: Secondary impacts extinguish remaining excess energy → Both drop to Blue (Low Speed).
• Red + Green: Both stay Green.
• Blue + Blue: Stable thermal ground collisions → Stay Blue.
  • The Relativity of Energy: If all particles are blue, there is no temperature contrast. Injecting 50 red particles injects a defined high-energy tier into the system.
  • The Quenching Cascade: Watch the chart when you press the red button. The red bar jumps instantly to 50, but within seconds, collisions rapidly drive it to zero while green spikes temporarily as an intermediate wave.
  • Statistical Inevitability: High-velocity particles sweep out larger collision cross-sections per second. Because every collision with a slower particle robs it of excess momentum, high-energy states decay far faster than they can ever be sustained, proving why low-energy states permanently dominate Boltzmann equilibrium.
