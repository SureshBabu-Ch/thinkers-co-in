Wien’s Distribution Law Calculator

Note on Classical Derivation: This calculation is performed purely according to Wilhelm Wien's original 1896 distribution law. It relies exclusively on classical thermodynamics, Wien's displacement theorem, and the Stefan–Boltzmann law. Planck’s quantum hypothesis ($E = nh\nu$) and Planck's constant ($h$) are not introduced here.
