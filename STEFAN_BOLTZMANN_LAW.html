Mathematical Proof & Spectral Breakdown

  • 1. Why the Bulb (2800 K) Curve Looked "Flat" Without Auto-Zoom: Because power scales with $T^4$, the peak emission at $6000\text{ K}$ is over $21\times$ taller than at $2800\text{ K}$. On a fixed global axis, $2800\text{ K}$ is compressed near the bottom. Clicking Auto-Zoom automatically rescales the vertical axis to the exact peak at any temperature so you can inspect its shape, peak location ($\lambda_{\text{max}} = 1035\text{ nm}$), and the rainbow overlap!
  • 2. Where the Light Actually Goes at 2800 K: As calculated in real time above, only ~9% of the total area falls inside the visible rainbow window ($380\text{ nm} - 750\text{ nm}$). Over 90% is radiated into the invisible infrared as heat.
  • 3. Integration to Stefan-Boltzmann: Integrating the Planck curve over all wavelengths from $0$ to $\infty$ yields:
    \text{Total Area} = \int_0^\infty E_\lambda(\lambda, T) \, d\lambda = \varepsilon \sigma T^4
