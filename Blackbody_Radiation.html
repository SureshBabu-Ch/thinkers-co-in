Microscopic: Cavity Mode Energy (Equipartition vs Quantum)
Formulas Used in Microscopic Simulation:
• Classical Equipartition: ⟨E⟩ = kB × T
Independent of frequency ν (every mode receives equal continuous thermal energy).
• Planck Quantum Oscillator: ⟨E⟩ = (h × ν) / [e(hν / kBT) - 1]
Energy quantization (E = nhν) suppresses high-frequency UV modes.
